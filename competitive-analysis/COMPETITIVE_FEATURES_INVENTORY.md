## THE BIG 5 MUST-HAVES (Universal — any Vertical)

Every client site MUST have these to compete with the market:

**1. MESSAGING FLOATING CTA** (87% competitors have it)
- Simple `wa.me/595XXXXXXXXX` or Messaging Business widget
- Floating bottom-right bubble
- This is non-negotiable in Paraguay

**2. ONLINE BOOKING FORM** (100% competitors have it)
- Minimum: Name, phone, service, preferred date
- Confirm via Messaging automatically
- Premium: real-time calendar slots

**3. SERVICE MENU WITH PRICES** (only 18% show prices)
- Huge opportunity — 82% DON'T show prices
- Show Gs. prices = transparency = trust = convert
- Package deals section

**4. INSTAGRAM / SOCIAL FEED** (91% have links = baseline)
- Embed Instagram feed on homepage
- Links are not enough — real content display
- Before/After posts in a gallery

**5. BEFORE/AFTER GALLERY** (only 9% have this)
- This is THE competitive advantage
- Almost nobody shows before/after
- Slider or side-by-side with swipe
- For: nails, tattoo, micropigmentation, facial, gym results, hair

## Website Technology Landscape

| Platform | # Competitors | Share |
|---------|--------------|-------|
| WordPress | 6 | 55% |
| Wix | 1 | 9% |
| Next.js | 2 | 18% |

**Insight:** 55% use WordPress — easy to migrate, host, operate.
Wix is rigid. Next.js = modern but more expensive to build.
Custom = probably expensive dev shops.

**Hosting infrastructure observations:**
- Most Paraguay sites use local hosting (supervision.com.py, hostinger.com.py)
- Cloudflare used as CDN by 1 competitor
- Meta Pixel for ads tracking is the dominant analytics (64%)
