# 1. PRICING MODEL OPTIONS

## 1.1 Free Website Model (Vete's Current Approach)

The free website model targets the 75% of businesses without websites. Revenue comes from:

### Revenue Streams
| Stream | Description | Potential |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| WhatsApp Business Premium | Enhanced features | $15-30/month |
| Featured Listings | Priority in directory | $10-20/month |
| Additional Services | Extra pages, e-commerce | $50-200 once |
| Featured Placement | "Sponsor" businesses | $20-50/month |

### Free Tier Features
- 1-page website
- Basic services listing
- WhatsApp integration
- Contact form
- Map location
- Basic SEO

### Premium Upsells
- Multi-page website (+3 pages): $150-300
- Portfolio gallery expansion: $50-100
- Online booking integration: $30-50/month
- E-commerce/shop: $100-300 setup + $30/month
- WhatsApp marketing: $15-25/month

# 4. PARAGUAY MARKET PRICING CONSIDERATIONS

## 4.1 Market Sensitivity

### Price Elasticity
- **Low sensitivity**: Spas, aesthetic clinics ($200+ acceptable)
- **Medium sensitivity**: Hair salons, gyms ($100-200 expected)
- **High sensitivity**: Nail salons, barbers ($50-100 expected)

### Competitive Positioning
- Free option: "Get your free website"
- $50-100: "Professional site, affordable"
- $150-200: "Premium, complete solution"
- $300+: "Enterprise, full service"

## 4.2 Payment Options

### Payment Plans
1. **Full payment** - 10% discount
2. **50/50** - 50% deposit, 50% on launch
3. **3 payments** - Monthly for 3 months
4. **Monthly retainer** - For ongoing work

### Paraguay-Specific
- Gs. (Guarani) pricing for local clients
- USD for international/tech clients
- Mercadopago integration for local payments
- Bank transfer options
