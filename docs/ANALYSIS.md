# Paraguay Beauty & Wellness Market Analysis

## Executive Summary

This analysis covers **7,463 beauty, wellness, and fitness businesses** across **209 cities in Paraguay**. The data reveals a massive market opportunity: **75% of businesses have no website**, and **97% are classified as Priority A or B leads** suitable for Vete/Paragu-AI's services.

**Key Finding**: The beauty and wellness market in Paraguay is underserved digitally. Most businesses operate via WhatsApp and Instagram only, with no professional web presence. This represents a significant opportunity for Vete's free website + WhatsApp booking offering.

### Total Market Size
- **7,463 businesses** identified

**Critical Insight**: Only 8% of beauty/wellness businesses in Paraguay have a real website. The remaining 92% operate via:

This is exactly Vete's target market — businesses that need professional websites but can't afford custom development.

## Technical Notes

### Data Collection
- **Source:** Google Maps Places API
- **Method:** Keyword-based text search + nearby search
- **Cities:** 46 primary cities searched, 209 total found
- **Keywords:** 17 per city (salon de belleza, peluqueria, spa, etc.)
- **Date:** April 14-15, 2026
- **API Requests:** ~2,500

### Scoring Algorithm
The deep score (0-100) considers:
- Website status (40 points max)
- Review count (30 points max)
- Rating (15 points max)
- Phone availability (10 points max)
- Category value (10 points max)
- Business size indicator (5 points max)
