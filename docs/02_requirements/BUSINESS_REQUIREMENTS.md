## 1. PELUQUERIA (Hair Salons)
**2,393 businesses | 81% no website | TOP PRIORITY**

### What They Need

**Essential Features:**
- Service catalog with prices (cuts, coloring, treatments)
- Online booking with date/time selection
- WhatsApp integration for confirmations
- Before/after photo gallery
- Staff profiles
- Appointment reminders
- Client loyalty program

**Optional Features:**
- Online store (hair products)
- Gift cards
- Recurring appointments
- Multi-location support

**Typical Services & Pricing:**
- Corte básico: Gs. 80,000-120,000
- Corte + Style: Gs. 100,000-150,000

- Mechas/Balayage: Gs. 200,000-400,000
- Alisado/Keratina: Gs. 250,000-500,000

- [ ] Popular hair trends in Paraguay

1. Home (hero image, CTA)
2. Servicios (catalog with prices)
3. Galería (portfolio)
4. Nuestro equipo (staff)
5. Reservas (booking widget)
6. Contacto (map, WhatsApp)
