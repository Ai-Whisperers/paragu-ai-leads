# Beauty & Wellness Website Feature Roadmap
## Based on First-World Competitor Analysis for Paraguay Market

---

## Overview

This document compiles findings from analyzing top-performing beauty, wellness, and fitness websites in the US, UK, and Europe. The goal is to identify essential and optional features for each business category to create a comprehensive feature roadmap for Vete/Paragu-AI.

**Data Foundation**: 7,463 Paraguay beauty/wellness businesses, 75% without websites

## Booking Platform Integration Recommendations

Based on competitor analysis, these are the most commonly used platforms in first-world markets:

### Primary Recommendations for Paraguay Market

1. **GlossGenius** - Popular for salons, includes marketing tools
2. **Fresha** - Free for businesses, widely used globally
3. **Square Appointments** - Good for small businesses, integrates with POS
4. **Vagaro** - Comprehensive for salons and fitness
5. **Mangomint** - Modern, clean interface for salons
6. **Mindbody** - For larger spas and fitness
7. **Calendly** - Simple booking, good for consultations

**Paraguay Adaptation**: Many businesses will need WhatsApp integration as primary booking method alongside any platform integration.

## Design Aesthetic Guidelines by Category

### Luxury/ Premium Positioning
- Dark theme with gold accents
- Serif typography
- Minimalist, generous whitespace
- High-quality photography

### Modern/ Trendy
- Bold colors or black/white
- Sans-serif typography
- Grid layouts
- Video integration

### Approachable/ Community
- Warm colors (earth tones, soft greens)
- Casual typography
- Testimonial-focused
- Clear CTAs

### Category-Specific Recommendations

| Category | Recommended Aesthetic |
|----------|----------------------|
| Hair Salon | Modern, bold, show-stopping visuals |
| Gym/Fitness | Energetic, clean, performance-focused |
| Spa/Wellness | Calming, nature-inspired, soft colors |
| Nail Salon | Clean, chic, art-focused |
| Tattoo | Dark, artistic, portfolio-first |
| Barber | Classic, sharp, masculine |
| Aesthetic Clinic | Clinical-clean or luxury dark |
| Makeup Artist | Portfolio-first, editorial feel |
| Hair Removal | Professional, clinical trust |

Based on competitor analysis, each website needs:

- Unique service page per treatment
- FAQ sections for search visibility
